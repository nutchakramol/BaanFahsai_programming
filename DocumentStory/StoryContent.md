# OVERALL STORY PROGRESSION

The player starts as a beginner home designer.

After successfully completing each customer's request, the player receives a new customer and unlocks new rooms and furniture.

The difficulty gradually increases:

- Level 1: Basic bedroom decoration
- Level 2: New bathroom and decoration
- Level 3: Larger social space and more decoration
- Level 4: Functional kitchen and more complex furniture arrangement
- Level 5: Highly customizable personal attic

The final goal is to complete all five customers' requests and become a successful home designer.
